Wi-Fi

Right now—

The board joins your home network, and everyone in the house opens it from there. It also finds your other Shelves.

The board makes its own Wi-Fi network, so The Shelf works anywhere: in the car, at camp, or where there's no Wi-Fi. Join it, then open http://192.168.4.1. Your phone will say "No Internet Connection": that's on purpose. It stays joined for The Shelf and keeps using cellular data for texts, iMessage and everything else.
